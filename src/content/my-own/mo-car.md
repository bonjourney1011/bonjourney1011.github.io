### Why I'm building it

A car is the second-biggest purchase most Vietnamese families make, yet the real price is hard to see. The listed price leaves out registration fees, plates and insurance that differ by province, and dealer discounts are negotiated one buyer at a time.

### What it will do

- **On-road price by province** — registration fee, plates, inspection and insurance added up for the buyer's own province.
- **Compare and choose** — side-by-side models and variants, with guides by budget, segment and need.
- **Fair dealer price bands** — only published once there are enough verified quotes from enough different dealers, always shown with sample size and date.

### The analyst behind it

Every number shows its source and freshness, and when the data is too thin the product says "not enough data" instead of guessing. It's the same discipline as my day job — applied to a business I own end to end, from market sizing and unit economics to the data model.
