## How to read it

1. **Sales overview** — revenue, orders and AOV, category share and the month-on-month change, with revenue against target.
2. **Finance / profitability** — gross profit and margin by category, target achievement by category × month, and price variance against list price.
3. **Supply chain / inventory** — SKUs below reorder point, stock coverage days and overdue payments by region, channel and customer.
