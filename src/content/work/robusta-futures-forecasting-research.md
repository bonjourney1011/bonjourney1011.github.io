## The question

Vietnam is the world's largest Robusta producer, so Robusta futures prices matter for farmers, traders and lenders alike. Many published forecasting comparisons quietly leak future information into training. We asked: **when every model family is evaluated fairly, which approach actually forecasts commodity futures best?**

## What we did

- Benchmarked **four model families** — statistical, machine learning, deep learning and hybrid — on ICE Robusta futures.
- Used a **fair walk-forward evaluation**: models are refit only on data available at each forecast date, which eliminates look-ahead bias.

## Publication

> "Fair Walk-Forward Forecasting of ICE Robusta Futures: Comparing Statistical, Machine-Learning, Deep-Learning and Hybrid Models." *Journal of Data Science and Intelligent Systems* (Scopus Q1, CiteScore 2025: 11.5). Accepted 09/2026.

## Why it matters for business analytics

The same discipline applies to any forecast a business acts on — demand, revenue, cash flow. A model that looks great in a leaky backtest fails in production, and the cost shows up in inventory and working capital.
